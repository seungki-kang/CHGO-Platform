# ADR-C02: Production Privacy Policy Baseline

- Status: Proposed
- Gate: C-02
- Decision: adopt the controls in `docs/privacy-security/C-02_Production_Privacy_Policy_Decision_Record_1.0.md` as the minimum production baseline.
- Consequence: production remains blocked until approval and regional evidence are complete.
- Canonical: unchanged; this ADR governs implementation and operations only.
- Supersession: a later approved ADR may change numeric periods or regional mechanisms, but cannot weaken Personal Vault ownership, Coach Dual Gate, Pastor Aggregate Only, raw-data isolation, or safety/privacy precedence.
